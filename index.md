## About Me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. I am advised by Professor Shengjun Huang and expect to graduate in 2027. My research focuses on computer vision and embodied intelligence.

## Research Interests

- **Computer Vision:** object detection and semi-supervised learning
- **Embodied Intelligence:** vision-language navigation, simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), and vision-language-action (VLA) models
